Salute to Rani Lakshmibai - The Warrior Queen of Jhansi

The Heroic Battle of Jhansi

Rani Lakshmibai of Jhansi, born as Manikarnika Tambe, was one of the leading figures of the Indian Rebellion of 1857. Known for her bravery, leadership, and fierce patriotism, she led her troops against the British forces with unmatched courage. Her legacy continues to inspire generations, symbolizing strength, sacrifice, and the undying spirit of freedom.
